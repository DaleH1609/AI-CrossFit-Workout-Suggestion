How should drag-to-fill work?

Three approaches — pick the one that feels most natural for building a schedule

A

Paint mode (recommended)

Click and hold on an empty cell → drag to paint classes on. Click and hold on an active cell → drag to erase. Direction is set by the first cell you touch. Like painting in MS Paint.

Rectangle select + confirm

Drag to draw a rectangular selection box. Release → a popup asks "Enable all?" or "Disable all?" You confirm before anything changes.

Shift+click range

Click the first cell, then Shift+click the last cell to fill the rectangular range between them. No dragging — works like selecting text.
